Καλό να ξέρετε The important details

Paying your fees.

How and when

  • Two instalments: 50% by 9 September 2025, the balance by 13 January 2026.
  • £20 early-payment discount if you settle the full year before the first day of school (9 September 2025).
  • A £20 late fee applies to each instalment paid after its due date.
  • Once enrolled (after the free trial), fees for the year are due in full — classes and teachers are allocated based on enrolled numbers at the start of the year.
  • GCSE exam entry fees are separate from the annual fee and payable before exam entries are submitted — we'll let you know the amount in good time.
  • Pay by cash, card, or bank transfer.
