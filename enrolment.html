Εγγραφές 2026/27

Joining is simple — and the first two weeks are free.

Register your child, let them settle in over a free two-week trial, and only then commit for the year. Here's everything you need to know.

Πώς λειτουργεί How it works

Three steps to a place.

Complete the registration form

Tell us about your child — their age, and any Greek they already have — using our online form. It takes about five minutes.

Registration form 2026/27

Two-week free trial

New children join their class for two Tuesdays completely free, so they can settle into their new surroundings before you decide.

Accept your place

Happy to continue? Your child is enrolled for the year and the first instalment of fees becomes due. That's it — welcome to the family.

Τα δίδακτρα Fees 2026/27

One annual fee. Books included.

Fees cover the full academic year, including textbooks and exercise books for every child (GCSE students bring their own A4 notepads). Multi-child discounts are built in.

Καλό να ξέρετε The important details

Paying your fees.

Payment options for 2026/27 are confirmed below.

How it works

  • Fees may be paid in full by Friday 28 August 2026, or in two instalments: the first by Friday 28 August 2026 and the second by Tuesday 5 January 2027.
  • Once enrolled (after the free trial), fees for the year are due in full — classes and teachers are allocated based on enrolled numbers at the start of the year.
  • GCSE examination entry fees are not included in the annual fee and are payable separately before examination entries are submitted.
  • Pay by cash, card, or bank transfer.
