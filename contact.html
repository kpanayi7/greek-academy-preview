Καλό να ξέρετε Drop-off and pick-up

Parking is limited — please walk your child in.

  • Parking at the school itself is limited, so please park on the main road or at the nearby Sainsbury's.
  • Walk your child to the designated Greek School entrance rather than dropping them at the gate — a committee member will guide you to the hall for assembly.
  • Please park considerately, keeping in mind residents and others crossing the road.
  • For pick-up, you may use the school car park, though it isn't very large. Please arrive in time to walk to the hall by 8pm, where teachers will bring the children.
