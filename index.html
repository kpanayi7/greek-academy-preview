Γιατί εμάς; Why families choose us

A proper school. A second family.

Run by a committee of parents and dedicated teachers, The Greek Academy gives children real progress in Greek — and real pride in where they come from.

📖

The language Η γλώσσα

A structured curriculum matched to each child's age and ability, following the same standards as other Greek schools in London — from first words in nursery all the way to a GCSE qualification.

💃

The culture Ο πολιτισμός

Dancing, plays, celebrations and traditions all year round — Christmas and 25th of March performances, and a summer play and BBQ to end the year together.

🤝

The community Η παρέα

Assembly and prayers bring the whole school together each week. Add the famous tuck shop at break time, and Tuesday quickly becomes the evening the children look forward to most.

Οι τάξεις μας Our classes

Η βραδιά μας A Tuesday at Greek school

Three hours, well spent.

5:00

Assembly & prayers

The whole school starts the evening together, led by our head teacher.

5:05

Lessons begin

Teachers escort each class to their room for the evening's learning.

6:30

Break & tuck shop

Snacks, water and pocket-money treats — often the highlight of the week.

8:00

Home time

Teachers bring the children back to the hall for pick-up.
