Ποιοι είμαστε Meet the committee

Eight parents, one school.

Our Parent Committee is essential to how The Greek Academy runs. You'll spot them every Tuesday at drop-off and pick-up, and they stay on throughout lessons — running the tuck shop, supporting teachers, supervising break time and, most importantly, helping the children. Every member holds an up-to-date DBS certificate.

Πέρα από τα μαθήματα What the committee does

More than the school day.

Beyond keeping Tuesday evenings running, the committee organises events throughout the year — dinner and dances and numerous fundraisers to support the school. Last year that meant pizza and hot dog nights, and a Halloween event with fancy dress and games.

Have a question or a concern? A committee member is always available for a chat in the hall once school has ended.
