Οι εκδηλώσεις μας

A school year full of Greek moments.

Plays, celebrations, and the biggest Greek night out in North London — here's what's coming up for our families.

Μην το χάσετε The big one

The TGA Dinner & Dance will be back.

Date to be announced

Dinner, music & the children on stage

Our biggest night of the year — a full evening for the whole family, with music, dancing and performances from our own students.

The committee is choosing a date for the next one. As soon as it is settled we will publish it here, along with ticket prices and how to book.

In the meantime, ask any committee member in the hall on a Tuesday evening, or email info@thegreekacademy.co.uk.

Όλη τη χρονιά Through the year

The dates every family circles.

15 December 2026

Christmas Play

The last Tuesday of the autumn term — the children take the stage for carols, sketches and Christmas cheer, Greek-style.

23 March 2027

25th of March Play

Celebrating Greek Independence Day with poems, songs and performances from every class — a night of real pride.

6 July 2027

Summer Play & BBQ

The final day of the school year. A performance from the children, then food and a proper Greek send-off into summer.

Twice a year

Parents' Evenings

Two designated evenings to sit down with your child's teacher and talk through their progress.

Every Tuesday

Assembly & prayers

The whole school together at 5pm sharp — the moment each Greek school evening begins.

Term time

Cultural programme

Every class takes a twenty-minute dance lesson each Tuesday, with Greek and Cypriot traditions woven through the year alongside the language lessons.
